Hospital Booking System

A booking, tracking and completion workflow for Vimut Hospital's IV Team, replacing phone calls, paper clipboards and manual Excel entry with one connected system.

Google Apps Script Google Sheets backend LINE Messaging API AI Conversation Booking Agent Live in production

Why build it

Departments across the hospital call in the IV Team for blood draws and IV insertions. Before this project, every request went through phone calls or chat messages, got scribbled onto a paper form, and was later retyped into an Excel sheet by hand. Nothing was centralized, so there was no easy way to see how fast requests were handled or why some got cancelled. We built one shared system to replace all of that.

Promo video

What we built

A Google Apps Script web app running on a single Google Sheet, with three pages: departments submit a request in seconds from Booking, IV nurses accept and complete cases from IV Team, and leadership watch live KPIs on the Dashboard. Every case logs its department, reason, patient details, timestamps, status and case owner automatically, and a LINE Messaging API integration notifies staff the moment a case is submitted, accepted or completed.

A phone-based AI voice agent is working: staff can call in a booking and have it filed automatically, with no retyping.

User flow

1 Department books By web, or by phone with the AI agent
→
2 IV Team notified LINE message or web notification
→
3 Case accepted IV nurse heads to the department
→
4 Case completed Procedure done at the bedside
→
5 Data recorded Logged automatically, no retyping
→
6 Dashboard updates Live, in real time

DonDon & Co.

A hand-built website, voice AI assistant and lead-automation pipeline for a friend's boutique agency.

Netlify hosting Voice AI agent Lead automation Live at dondon.agency

Why build it

A friend's agency needed a real website: something professional to be their main digital presence and bring in leads. What started as a straightforward six-page site grew into a fuller stack once we got going, covering hosting, a voice AI assistant, and automated lead handling behind the scenes.

What we built

Six pages, hand-coded: Home, About, Services, Team, Clients and Contact, built from the agency's real brand assets (logo, team photos, 27 client logos) with copy pulled straight from their credential deck. The Services page is structured around a five-stage process: Discover, Define, Design, Communicate, Transform.

Under the hood: structured data and a sitemap for search engines, a zero-backend contact form, Google Analytics with a PDPA-compliant cookie banner, and scroll animations built so the content stays fully crawlable.

A voice AI assistant sits on every page, built only from the site's real content with rules against inventing facts about the agency. Behind the scenes, an automation script picks up leads from both the voice assistant and the contact form, logs them to a spreadsheet, researches each one with AI, and emails the team a summary.

Condo AI Chatbot

A LINE account that calculates parking fees automatically and answers residents' questions about the condo.

LINE Official Account AI chatbot Parking fee calculator Google Apps Script

Why build it

At my previous condo, the security guards kept miscalculating the parking fee by hand. I built a LINE account that did the math automatically, and added an AI chatbot on top so residents could also ask it questions about the condo.

What we built

A parking fee calculator built into the LINE account: enter a license plate and the entry and exit time, and it works out the fee automatically, including discounts for a juristic stamp or an owner's signature. No more manual math at the gate.

On top of that, an AI chatbot answers questions about the condo itself: the project details, how many floors, what room types are available. For anything outside that, it can search the internet, but it's scoped to only answer questions related to the condo, and declines anything else.

User flow

1 Message the LINE account A question, or the menu
→
2 Ask about the condo Answered from condo info or a web search
→
3 Or calculate parking Open the fee calculator
→
4 Enter plate and times Plus any applicable discount
→
5 Fee appears instantly No manual math at the gate

Product screenshots

How it helped

The account ran in real use for a few months, helping the security team and hundreds of residents calculate parking fees with little effort and no more manual mistakes at the gate. It was eventually retired after the juristic company upgraded to a dedicated parking-fee machine.
